# E-Commerce Sales Insight Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce sales, customer behavior, and refund patterns using an interactive dashboard.  
The goal is to uncover revenue drivers, customer trends, regional performance, and operational issues that impact growth and customer experience.

---

## Objectives
- Analyze total sales and order performance
- Identify top-performing products and sales channels
- Track sales growth by year and month
- Understand customer distribution and loyalty trends
- Analyze refund patterns and reasons
- Highlight data quality or logic issues where applicable

---

## Dataset Description
The dataset contains e-commerce transaction and customer data, including:
- Sales and order values
- Product categories
- Sales channels (Website, Direct Sales, Resellers, Marketplace, Partner)
- Customer type (New vs Loyal)
- Country-level performance
- Refund reasons and refunded orders
- Time-based sales metrics (monthly and yearly)

*Dataset used for analytical and visualization purposes.*

---

---

## Key Insights
- Total sales reached **$22.45M**, driven mainly by **website and direct sales channels**.
- High-performing products include **Microsoft 365 Business** and **Azure AI Studio**.
- **2025 contributed over 54% of total revenue**, indicating strong year-over-year growth.
- The **United States** leads in both sales and customer count.
- Refunds are primarily caused by **billing errors** and **accidental purchases**, suggesting opportunities to improve checkout and billing processes.
- While customer growth is steady at **4,000 customers**, the **100% loyalty rate indicates a potential data or logic issue** that should be reviewed.

---

## Visualizations Included
- KPI summary cards (Sales, Orders, Customers, AOV)
- Sales by product and sales channel
- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Year-over-year sales growth
- Sales and customers by country
- Refunds by reason and country
- New vs loyal customer distribution

---

## Business Recommendations
- Optimize website and direct sales channels to sustain revenue growth.
- Review billing and checkout processes to reduce refund rates.
- Validate customer loyalty calculations to ensure accurate reporting.
- Expand high-performing product offerings and regional strategies in the U.S.
- Monitor refund trends to improve customer experience and retention.

---

## Conclusion
This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of e-commerce performance by combining sales, customer, and operational insights. It enables stakeholders to make informed decisions, identify growth opportunities, and address potential data quality issues.
